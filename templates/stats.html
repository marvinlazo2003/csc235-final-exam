🌪️ Scary Tornado Facts

Tornadoes are some of the most violent storms on Earth.

Tornadoes vary in time

Once a tornado has touched down. It can remain on the ground for a few minutes to hours.

Tornado sirens are recent.

Tornado warning sirens only have been invented during the 1970s. Meaning that prior to the 70s. Nobody would've able to have a quick enough warning to escape or seek shelter in time.

Nocturnal Tornadoes

Yes you read that right. Tornadoes don't discriminate on the time of day. They sometimes touch down during the middle of the night so people could be fast asleep and not realize the tornado sirens and the storm hitting the ground.

Widest ever recorded tornado reached over 2 miles in width

The 2013 El Reno tornado reached 2.6 miles wide. That's more than double the entire campus of Southern!

It isn't the tornado itself that kills you.

Even small tornadoes have enough power to pick people up. What makes them lethal for the most part is people getting hit by debris of various sizes at high speeds.

Tornado standing still... Or is it?

If you're viewing a tornado and it's seeming not moving. That means it's either coming towards you or away.
