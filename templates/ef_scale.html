Enhanced Fujita (EF) Scale

EF0 (65–85 mph)

Light damage: tree branches broken, minor roof damage.

EF1 (86–110 mph)

Moderate damage: roofs stripped, mobile homes damaged.

EF2 (111–135 mph)

Considerable damage: roofs torn off, trees uprooted.

EF3 (136–165 mph)

Severe damage: entire floors of buildings destroyed.

EF4 (166–200 mph)

Devastating damage: houses leveled, cars thrown.

EF5 (>200 mph)

Incredible damage: complete destruction of structures.
