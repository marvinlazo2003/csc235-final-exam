Tornado Safety Tips
  • Never chase tornadoes - Leave storm chasing to trained professionals
  • Seek shelter immediately if a tornado warning is issued for your area
  • Go to the lowest floor of a sturdy building, away from windows
  • Cover your head with your arms and get under sturdy furniture if possible
  • If outdoors, lie flat in a ditch or low-lying area and cover your head
  • Never try to outrun a tornado in a vehicle on congested roads
